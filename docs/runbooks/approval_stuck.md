# Approval waiting over 15 minutes

A request is blocking an agent.

## Steps
1. Open Approvals (desktop or phone) and approve or deny.
2. Unanswered Tier-3 requests expire after 15 minutes and are denied by default.

## Escalate
If this does not fix it, ask Jasper (it can read `alerts`) or Incident Response; record the cause in `docs/ops/` when it was not obvious.
