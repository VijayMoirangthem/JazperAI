# Human Organization

Humans use the same org model as agents but may not appear on the pixel floor.

## Representation

- **Owner / executives** — often human; may use Ask Me or calendar integrations.
- **Employees** — `agent_type` or registry flag distinguishes human vs AI.
- **Escalations** — hive messages with `needsHuman` route envelopes to the office entrance tile.

## Permissions

Humans typically hold higher authority: approve spend, hire/fire, change org structure via Manage org UI.

## Freelancers

External workers attached to **projects** (temporary cross-functional structures). Not assigned a permanent desk by default; may appear in org chart under project teams.
